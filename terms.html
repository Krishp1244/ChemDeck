1. About ChemDeck

ChemDeck ("the app", "we", "us") is a free flashcard study tool for organic chemistry, built and maintained as an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of any university, publisher, exam board, or employer.

The app runs entirely in your browser. It is hosted as a static site, and it uses Google Firebase for sign-in and for storing your flashcards.

3. Your account and email

ChemDeck has no passwords of its own. You sign in with Google, and Google tells the app three things about you: a unique account identifier, your email address, and (if you have one set) your display name and profile picture. ChemDeck never sees your Google password.

Why we confirm your email

On your first sign-in, the app shows you the email address Google returned and asks you to confirm it is yours before any flashcards are created. This exists for two reasons. It stops you from quietly building a set of cards under a second Google account you did not mean to use (easy to do on a shared or school computer), and it makes the account your data is tied to explicit rather than assumed.

The app also requires that the address is one Google has verified. If Google reports the address as unverified, or returns no address at all, sign-in is refused and you are signed straight back out.

Keeping the account secure

You are responsible for the security of the Google account you sign in with. Anyone who can use that Google account can read, change, and delete your flashcards. If you sign in on a device that is not yours, sign out when you are finished.

11. Privacy notice

What is collected

  • Account identity. Your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile picture URL, supplied by Google when you sign in.
  • Your study content. Card fronts and backs, drawings, scratchpad images, deck and folder names, and their ordering.
  • Your consent record. The terms version you accepted and the time you accepted it.

There is no analytics, no advertising network, no session recording, no fingerprinting, and no third party tracker in this app. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell or share personal data with anyone for their own purposes.

Why, and on what basis

Account identity is processed to authenticate you and to keep your cards separated from everyone else's. Study content is processed to provide the service you asked for. The consent record is processed to demonstrate that terms were accepted. In UK and EU terms, the basis is performance of a contract with you, and, for the consent record, our legitimate interest in keeping a record of it.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your account record and your cards, and acts as our processor. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • GitHub Pages serves the static files. GitHub records server access logs, including IP addresses, under its own policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the Inter typeface, which means your browser requests a file from fonts.gstatic.com.

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and may be processed outside your country, under Google's standard contractual safeguards.

How long it is kept

Your cards are kept until you delete them or until your account is deleted. The account record is kept while the account exists. Deleting a card removes it from cloud storage; backups held by the infrastructure provider may persist for a short period afterwards under their own retention schedule.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to it. Most of this you can do yourself inside the app. For anything else, use the contact route in section 14. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

ChemDeck is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child's data is held here, contact us and it will be removed.

12. Cookies and local storage

ChemDeck does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Everything it stores on your device is required for the app to function, which is why the consent screen has one button rather than a set of toggles. If you do not want any of it stored, the honest answer is not to use the app.

What is stored on your device

  • Firebase Authentication session. Set by Google and Firebase in browser storage and cookies on Google's own domains, so that you stay signed in between visits. Strictly necessary. Persists until you sign out or clear site data.
  • chemdeck-theme (local storage). Whether you chose light or dark. Persists until cleared.
  • chemdeck-sidebar (local storage). Whether the sidebar is open. Persists until cleared.
  • chemdeck-cookie-consent (local storage). The version of this notice you accepted, so you are not asked again.
  • chemdeck-terms-accepted:<account id> (local storage). A local copy of your terms acceptance, so the app does not have to query the server on every load.

Clearing it

Clearing site data for this site in your browser settings removes all of the above. You will be asked to accept this notice again, and to sign in again. Your flashcards are unaffected, because they live in cloud storage, not on your device.

13. Security

Your cards are protected by per user Cloud Firestore rules: a signed in account can read and write only the documents under its own account identifier, and the shape and size of every field is validated server side. Content is served over HTTPS with a content security policy that restricts where scripts, styles, images, and network connections may come from.

No system is perfectly secure, and this one is maintained by one person. If you find a vulnerability, please report it privately rather than publicly, and do not access, modify, or delete data belonging to anyone else while testing. Reports made in good faith are welcome and will not be pursued. The reporting route and the current security posture are documented in SECURITY.md in the project repository.
